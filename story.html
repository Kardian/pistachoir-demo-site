01 · The shop

A corner, painted blue.

The shop on Larkin Street was a locksmith for forty years. Teo painted it cobalt the week he got the keys, because he wanted it to be the first thing you see from the harbour.

02 · The morning

One machine, twelve batches.

The batch freezer runs from seven until the last tray is full. Each flavour is made from scratch: roast, grind, cook, chill, churn.

03 · The rule

Gone is gone.

No freezer full of backups. If pistachio sells out at four, the next pistachio is tomorrow at seven. Reserve a tub if you can't wait.
