# Credits

## Photos
All eight photos in `img/` (hero, cups, tray, ingredients, maker, storefront, counter, coupe) were generated on 2026-11-21 with Grok Bot's built-in image generator, in one direct-flash phone-photo style. They are AI-generated images of an invented shop and an invented person, not photos of a real place or person, and they contain no text. The counter photo is cropped to remove a shelf whose bottle labels looked like lettering. No stock photos are used.
